    Documents

    Downloads separate PDFs with their original receipt filenames. Allow multiple downloads in your browser. If blocked, use the ZIP fallback for one download or the individual buttons below.

      Retry is available only when RingCentral reports a definitive fax failure. Unknown outcomes cannot be retried here.

      ⓘ Fax sending info

      A fax is marked Sent only when RingCentral reports the final Sent status. Checks stop after 15 minutes; unresolved faxes remain Status Unknown. Sent does not confirm human receipt or review.

      Keep this tab open while sending. Documents stay in this tab only. Clear All or reloading removes this document list and its duplicate protection. Recent attempt metadata remains in Fax History.

      Fax History
