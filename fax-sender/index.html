Fax Sender

Select PDFs and send each document as a separate fax through RingCentral.

    Up to 4 MB per PDF. Each PDF is a separate fax. No automatic cover page is added. Selecting more files adds them to the list.

    Documents

      Retry is available only when RingCentral reports a definitive fax failure. Unknown outcomes cannot be retried here.

      ⓘ Fax sending info

      A fax is marked Sent only when RingCentral reports the final Sent status. Checks stop after 15 minutes; unresolved faxes remain Status Unknown. Sent does not confirm human receipt or review.

      Keep this tab open while sending. Documents and results stay in this tab only. Clear All or reloading removes this list and its duplicate protection.
